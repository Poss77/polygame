Privacy Policy

Data Protection & Transparency Commitment for PolyBot

Privacy Commitment: PolyBot collects only the minimal data required to process your commands, manage your balance vault, and track arcade tournament scores. We never sell your data, we never collect private keys, and we do not track external browsing activity.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with PolyBot in Discord or use the wallet connection portal, we collect the following minimal data points:

  • Discord Identifiers: Your Discord User Snowflake ID and Username. This is required to identify your account, calculate faucet streaks, and attribute your in-bot balance.
  • Public Wallet Address: When you voluntarily verify your wallet via /link, we store your public Polygon EVM address (e.g., 0x1234...).
  • Transaction & Audit Logs: Cryptographic records of balance deposits, withdrawals, tips, tournament winnings, and faucet claims to ensure ledger integrity and auditability.
  • Arcade Scores: High scores, game sessions, and timestamps generated on polygongaming.io associated with your linked wallet or player ID.
  • Guild Identifiers: Discord Server (Guild) IDs and channel IDs where commands are invoked, used solely to route command responses and apply server-specific faucet configurations.

2. What We NEVER Collect

In accordance with industry Web3 security standards, PolyBot does NOT collect or store:

  • Private keys, seed phrases, or wallet passwords.
  • Real names, physical addresses, or phone numbers.
  • Payment card numbers or banking credentials.
  • Direct messages or server messages unrelated to PolyBot slash commands or button interactions.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used strictly for operational purposes:

  • To maintain and update your social vault balances (PGT, POL, and supported tokens).
  • To verify wallet ownership using Sign-In with Ethereum (EIP-191 cryptographic signatures).
  • To score and distribute tournament prizes to top leaderboard finishers.
  • To prevent automated sybil abuse, spam, and botting on community faucets.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We respect your privacy and enforce strict data sharing rules:

  • No Sale of Data: We will never sell, rent, monetize, or lease your personal information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.
  • Public Blockchains: When you perform an on-chain withdrawal, the transaction hash, destination wallet, and token amount are permanently and publicly broadcast to the Polygon blockchain by nature of distributed ledger technology.
  • Infrastructure Providers: Data is hosted securely on enterprise cloud infrastructure (Supabase PostgreSQL with Row Level Security enabled).

5. Data Security & Storage

We employ robust security defenses to protect your information:

  • All Discord webhook interactions are cryptographically verified via Ed25519 digital signatures.
  • Database tables are protected by strict PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) policies and least-privilege execution roles.
  • Wallet linkages require an active cryptographic nonce challenge to prevent replay attacks.

6. Data Retention & Right to be Forgotten

We retain your profile data for as long as your account actively utilizes PolyBot. If you wish to unlink your wallet or delete your off-chain Discord profile data, you may request deletion at any time by contacting our community administration in Discord. Upon verification, your off-chain profile and linked wallet mapping will be purged from our active database.
